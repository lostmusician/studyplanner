# NUS programme catalogue and requirement coverage

Catalogue reviewed on 5 November 2026. The primary curricula remain the five NUS College computing programmes already supported by this planner.

## Official directories

- [NUS minors](https://nus.edu.sg/registrar/academic-information-policies/undergraduate-students/special-programmes/minor-programmes): includes the published Semester 2 AY2026/27 list. Criminal Justice is labelled as a future offering.
- [NUS second majors](https://www.nus.edu.sg/registrar/academic-information-policies/undergraduate-students/special-programmes/double-major-programmes).
- [FASS language minors](https://fass.nus.edu.sg/discover/second-majors-minors/): individual languages expand the registrar's Language Studies umbrella.
- [NUS double degrees](https://www.nus.edu.sg/registrar/academic-information-policies/undergraduate-students/special-programmes/double-degree-programmes): 15 specially designed combination families. Business Administration/Accountancy and Engineering combinations are families, not an assertion that every pairing is permissible.
- [Double-degree framework](https://www.nus.edu.sg/registrar/academic-information-policies/undergraduate-students/special-programmes/double-degree-programmes/double-degree-programme-framework-and-guidelines).

The directory exposes 109 minor choices and 64 second-major choices, including individual language minors and instrument choices under the music umbrella. Duplicate cross-faculty listings are consolidated. Chinese Studies/Chinese Language use the registrar's labels; faculty pages may use newer bilingual/cultural labels. China Studies combines the FASS/NUSC directory listings; its partner-university substitution rules require manual review.

Featured choices are editorial shortcuts retained from the previously supported programmes, not a measured popularity ranking. No enrolment statistics were used.

## Course-check coverage

All authored secondary programmes are explicitly marked **partial**. Directory-only programmes are **review-needed**, have no invented course requirements, and never display a completion percentage or a passing overlap chip. Selecting a programme records a planning intention, not an APAD declaration or an admission decision.

This update audits the Mathematics minor and Statistics/Data Analytics second majors against the linked cohort PDFs and adds subject-coded course routes for History, Philosophy and Sociology second majors. Each includes its source URL. Existing secondary course rules remain available but are not represented as fully verified. Residency, grades, equivalent/cross-listed course preclusions, substitutions, department approvals and all permitted combinations cannot be completely checked with the current student-plan data.

Statistics and Data Analytics now support actual units, the MA1511 + MA1512 alternative, the 2-unit MA1513 route and its additional elective. Statistics excludes ST328x and ST4288 and does not reuse the computing alternative as an advanced elective. CS3244/CS3264 count once as a Data Analytics elective option. The Mathematics minor follows the published advanced-course list and excludes the named UROPS/project courses. Dummy MAx99x codes and cross-listings still need manual review.

Known CS/home-faculty Computing exclusions and same-subject minor/second-major conflicts are disabled in the selector. This is not a comprehensive NUS eligibility engine. Imported older plans can contain combinations that need review.

## Double degrees

A selected DDP is saved, shared, restored in review mode and printed separately from the second major. The panel records the current university framework, links to official faculty requirements, and explicitly says degree-specific graduation checks are unavailable. It does not apply the minor/second-major 40% overlap cap to double degrees, change a single-degree graduation target, invent a combined curriculum, or claim the student has completed two degrees. Overseas joint/concurrent programmes and arbitrary student-initiated combinations are outside this catalogue.

Full DDP graduation validation requires faculty/cohort-specific combined curricula, approved overlap mappings, separate degree GPAs, NUS residency and permission records. These remain outstanding work.

## Verification

Automated checks cover catalogue merging, exclusions, unit totals, elective allocation, equivalent alternatives, and compact sharing. Browser checks cover narrow-header geometry, programme search and local-storage persistence. Real-device checks are still needed for touch behavior and keyboard/viewport behavior.

## Global Pathways (multi-select)

Global Pathways are now stored as `specialProgrammes`; legacy single-value and NOC-only plans/links migrate through one shared normalizer. An explicit new empty array always wins over legacy values. Reset, import/review, major switching, advisor undo, printing and compact sharing preserve the array. Selecting a new NOC duration replaces the previous one while retaining other pathways.

The one unmet item in each shipped sample plan was its undeclared Global Pathway. The visualiser now lists outstanding requirements and links directly to Pathways settings. Choosing an experience fulfils the *planned pathway* requirement, not course credit.

MCE credit uses actual placed/waived courses or explicit approved mappings. GEx contributes through an NEX course once, with no selection bonus. NFB is HSS, NFC is ST, and NHT Creative is neither domain. SEP selection neither awards courses nor relaxes domain minimums. Mappings are limited to two per semester abroad for the standard route and one under the currently supported NOC PWW route. Extra mappings do not invalidate a plan that has enough other eligible courses.

NOC 6-month/1-year reduces the six-MCE target by 2/4 and retains the domain and taught-course minimums. The waiver helper uses the highest entitlement, capped at four, rather than adding overlapping waivers. Other degree-specific PWW entitlements are not inferred from the DDP catalogue. Faculty/industry targets are no longer reduced or marked fulfilled solely by selecting NOC; approved internship courses are checked against the major's rules.
